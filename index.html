📖 How to play

  1. Add a unicorn for every player (up to 6) and give it a name and colour.
  2. On your turn, tap Play to start a Kindness Quest of 10 questions.
  3. Get 7 or more right to win your points and a surprise squishy.
  4. Then play 🌈 Rainbow Run: tap to jump, tap again in the air to double jump. Collect sparkles and bounce on grumpy clouds!
  5. Pass the device to the next player. Keep playing to level up your unicorn!

Types of points

⭐Stars 10 for every correct answer
💖Kindness Hearts kind-heart questions answered right (5 pts each)
🌈Rainbow Gems every 3 correct in a row (15 pts each)
👑Golden Crown a perfect 10 out of 10 (50 pts)
✨Sparkles collected in Rainbow Run (1 pt each)
🧸Squishies a surprise squishy for each quest you pass. Collect all 16!
